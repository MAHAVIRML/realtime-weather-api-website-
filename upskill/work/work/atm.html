Card Reading and Account Verification

Card reading and account verification are essential components of ATM functionality, ensuring secure and accurate transactions. When a user inserts their card, the ATM reads the magnetic stripe or embedded chip, which contains encrypted data unique to the cardholder, such as the account number. The ATM then communicates with the bank’s network to authenticate the card details by cross-referencing them with the bank’s database. After the card is read, the user enters their Personal Identification Number (PIN), which provides an additional layer of security. The encrypted PIN is sent to the bank’s server for verification. If the PIN matches, the user gains access to their account and can proceed with transactions, preventing unauthorized access.
